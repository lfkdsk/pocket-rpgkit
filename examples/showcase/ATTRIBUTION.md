The generated PNG, PKTS, JSON, and TypeScript manifests are deterministic
build outputs. Re-run `bun examples/showcase/gen-assets.ts` after changing
the project or source art. The structured inventory in `tuxemon-assets.ts`
and its test ensure that every copied Tuxemon file is listed here and uses an
allowed license.
