# Feature Gallery art

All map textures, character sprites, portraits, effects, and battle sprites
in this example are deterministic geometric art generated by
`examples/showcase/gen-assets.ts` for Pocket RPG Kit. They are distributed
under the repository's MIT license.

The checked-in PNG, PKTS, JSON, and TypeScript manifests are generated
outputs. Re-run `bun examples/showcase/gen-assets.ts` after changing the
project or its procedural palette.
